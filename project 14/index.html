Plants belong to the kingdom Plantae and are multicellular eukaryotic organisms characterized by their ability to perform photosynthesis.

This process allows them to convert sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide into glucose and oxygen, making them primary producers in ecosystems.

There are an estimated 400,000 different species of plants known to science, and new species are continually being described, particularly from tropical areas of the world.

Plants play a vital role in the maintenance of life on Earth. All energy used by living organisms depends on the complex process of photosynthesis, which is mostly carried out by green plants.
