Life After Death

Life after death is a fundamental belief in most religions.
Some people without a religious belief also believe in life after death and some believe that there is no sort of existence after death.
The belief that some part of a person (perhaps their energy) passes into a new life form after death.
Christian belief that Jesus rose from the dead after the crucifixion; the rising from the dead of believers on the Last Day; the new, or risen, life of Christians.



The belief that after death there will be nothing. This is a view held by many atheists.
