Pollution

Definition: Pollution is the introduction of harmful substances into the environment, which can be natural or man-made.These substances, known as pollutants, contaminate air, water, and land.

Types of Pollution: The main types of pollution include:

Air Pollution: Caused by emissions from vehicles, industries, and burning fossil fuels, leading to respiratory diseases and climate change.

Water Pollution: Resulting from industrial waste, sewage, and agricultural runoff, which can harm aquatic life andcontaminate drinking water.

Land Pollution: Occurs due to the disposal of waste, chemicals, and plastics, affecting soil quality and food safety.

Noise pollution:Noise pollution, also known as sound pollution or acoustic pollution, is the propagation of noise that disrupts the natural environment and can lead to adverse health effects.
